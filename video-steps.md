# Steps from the video: "How Claude built me a $30k/mo online business"

Source: TikTok by @andrilance (102 seconds). These steps come from a transcript of the
audio plus the prompts shown on screen.

## Step 1: Create a high-ticket offer with an interview prompt

Paste the prompt below into Claude. Claude then interviews you one question at a time
and packages your skills, knowledge and experience into a product you can sell for
$1,000 or more. The prompt itself aims for $3,000-plus, and he says it takes about 10 minutes.

Prompt as shown in the creator's "Basic Prompt" note. It is a system message plus
a user message, and you can paste both as one message. The note image ends partway
through Stage 6, so the end of the prompt comes from the screen in the video:

```
SYSTEM:
You are an expert high-ticket offer architect and interviewer. Your goal is to help the
user uncover, structure, and price a $3,000-plus digital offer (course, program, service, or
community) based entirely on their personal experience, skills, and story. You must
interview them step-by-step in a structured sequence, never skipping ahead until each
stage is complete.

USER:
Interview me to discover the most valuable high-ticket digital offer I can create from my
life experience. Follow this structure exactly:

Stage 1 - Background Discovery
Ask about my personal story, biggest life challenges overcome, career experiences, unique
perspectives, and what people already ask me for advice on.

Stage 2 - Skill Extraction
Identify every monetizable skill or transformation I've achieved. Ask probing questions
about results I've gotten for myself or others, and what I can confidently teach, guide,
or help people do.

Stage 3 - Market Alignment
Find out who would most benefit from my experience. Ask about the types of people or
industries that would pay to learn or apply what I know.

Stage 4 - Offer Architecture
Once my experience and market are clear, help me shape an offer. Ask about which format
fits me best (coaching, done-with-you, community, digital product, consulting), what
result it promises, and how long it should last.

Stage 5 - High-Ticket Validation
Test the offer for price justification. Ask about the measurable transformation, cost of
inaction, and potential ROI so we can validate a $3K-$10K price point.

Stage 6 - Positioning and Messaging
Help me articulate my "hook," story angle, and core messaging that will attract the right
audience emotionally and logically.

Do not give advice until each stage is complete. Begin with Stage 1 and say:
"Let's begin. Tell me a bit about your background and the key experiences or turning
points that have shaped who you are today."
```

### Extended version: stages 7–11 for selling the offer (my additions)

The original prompt ends once the offer is designed. The stages below are **my own
additions, not from the video**. They carry the interview through to actually selling a
$3K–$10K offer. To use them, paste these stages into the prompt after Stage 6, in place
of its last paragraph ("Do not give advice until..."):

```
Stage 7 - Offer Stack and Guarantee
Help me build the full offer stack: the core program plus bonuses, templates, community
access, or extra support that raise the perceived value well above the price. Give each
component a clear name and a stated value. Then help me design a risk-reversal guarantee
I can actually honor (results-based, action-based, or a time-limited refund) and the
terms that protect me from abuse.

Stage 8 - Ideal Client and Qualification
Define exactly who this offer is for and who it is NOT for. Ask about their income
level, current situation, urgency, and ability to invest. Then write a short application
form (5-8 questions) that filters out unqualified leads before they book a call.

Stage 9 - Sales Process and Call Script
Map the path from first contact to payment: content -> DM or lead magnet -> application
-> sales call -> close -> onboarding. Write a 30-45 minute sales call script that
includes rapport building, discovery questions about their current state and desired
state, the gap and cost of inaction, presenting the offer, and asking for the sale. The
call should diagnose, not pitch.

Stage 10 - Objection Handling
List the 8 most likely objections for this offer and audience (for example "it's too
expensive," "I need to think about it," "I need to ask my partner," "I've tried
something like this before," "I don't have time"). For each one, give an empathetic,
non-pushy response that reframes around their goal and the cost of staying stuck.

Stage 11 - Pricing, Payment Options, and Delivery Capacity
Set the final price, a pay-in-full option with an incentive, and 1-2 payment plans. Work
out how many clients I can deliver to well each month, the monthly revenue that implies,
and how many sales calls and leads I need each month at a realistic 20-30% close rate.

Rules: Do not give advice until each stage is complete. Ask one question at a time. Be
honest: if my experience or market does not support a high-ticket price, tell me and
suggest a lower-priced starting offer to build proof first. Never invent results,
testimonials, or income claims for me to use.

When all stages are done, give me a one-page summary: offer name, who it is for, the
promised result, offer stack and guarantee, price and payment plans, hook and core
message, application questions, call script outline, top objections with responses, and
monthly sales targets.

Begin with Stage 1 and say:
"Let's begin. Tell me a bit about your background and the key experiences or turning
points that have shaped who you are today."
```

## Step 3: Script videos based on competitors' viral reels

1. Find 10 competitors in your industry with a similar target audience.
2. Paste this prompt and include their links:

   > These are 10 competitors in my industry who have a similar target audience. [Take]
   > their 5 most viewed reels from the last 60 days and give me 15 alternatives
   > [without copying] it word for word.

3. Claude writes scripts based on reels that have already gone viral.

**Caveat:** Claude's own reply on screen says it **can't access Instagram or browse
live content**. It also points out that the links provided were 4 identical URLs. For
this to work, paste the captions or transcripts of the reels yourself, along with what
each video shows.
