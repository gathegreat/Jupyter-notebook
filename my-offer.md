## Stage 5: Price validation
- **Cost of doing nothing:** years of releasing into silence, money wasted on
  ineffective promo, and paying for "networking events" that never lead anywhere.
- **Value:** one strong industry relationship can lead to a sync deal, management, a
  booking or a job, and any one of those is worth far more than £2,500.
- **Honest check:** you don't yet have documented client results. Before charging £2,500:
  - Run a **pilot cohort of 5–8 at £1,200**.
  - Collect testimonials and outcomes (meetings booked, gigs, signings, jobs).
  - Then raise the price.

**Guarantee (action-based):** attend every session and complete every assignment. If you

place on the next cohort. Don't promise signings or deals: you can't control those.

## Stage 8: Who it's for and how to qualify them
**For:** people already making music or actively trying to work in the industry, who
can commit 3–4 hours a week and can invest.
**Not for:** anyone looking for a guaranteed record deal, people who aren't releasing
or working on anything yet, and anyone unwilling to network.

**Application questions:**
1. Are you an artist or an aspiring industry professional? What's your sound or role?
2. Link to your music or socials, or describe your experience so far.
3. What have you tried already to get noticed or get in?
4. Where do you want to be in 12 months?
5. What's stopping you right now?
6. Can you commit 3–4 hours a week for 8 weeks?
7. This programme costs £2,500, with payment plans available. Are you in a position to
   invest in your career right now?

## Stage 9: Sales process and call outline
**Path:** content → DM keyword or free guide ("5 things A&Rs look for") → application
→ 30–45 min call → payment → onboarding.

**Call outline:**
1. **Rapport (5 min):** their music or role, and how they found you.
2. **Current state (10 min):** what they're doing now and what's not working.
3. **Desired state (5 min):** where they want to be and why it matters.
4. **The gap (5 min):** what it costs them to stay where they are for another year.
5. **Present (10 min):** walk through the 8 weeks and the showcase, tied to *their* goals.
6. **Ask (5 min):** "Based on everything you've told me, do you want to do this?"

## Stage 10: Objection handling
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive." | "Compared with what? How much have you spent on promo that didn't work? This puts you in front of people who can actually move your career." |
| "I need to think about it." | "Of course. What specifically do you need to think through? Let's look at it now while it's fresh." |
| "I need to ask my partner or parents." | "Makes sense. What do you think they'll ask? Let's make sure you have the answers." |
| "Will I get signed?" | "I can't promise that, and anyone who does is lying. I can promise you'll be ready and in the room." |
| "I don't have time." | "It's 3–4 hours a week. How many hours a week go into making music right now that nobody hears?" |
| "I've tried courses before." | "What was missing? Most courses stop at theory. This one ends with a real showcase in front of industry guests." |
| "I'm not ready yet." | "Being ready comes from doing this, not before it. What would ready look like to you?" |
| "Can I just come to the showcase?" | "The showcase works because people arrive prepared. That's what the 8 weeks are for." |

## Stage 11: Pricing and capacity
- **Pilot:** 5–8 people at £1,200, to get proof.
- **Full price:** £2,500 pay in full (includes a bonus 1:1 session), or 3 × £950.
- **Capacity:** 10–12 people per cohort, one cohort every ~2 months.
- **Revenue per cohort:** about £25,000–£30,000, roughly **£12,500–£15,000 a month**.
- **Sales maths:** at a 25% close rate, 10 clients means about 40 calls, which means
  about 80 applications per cohort.
